Economists assumed people would abandon their jobs for a guaranteed stipend. The villagers kept working. Researchers watched 100 other communities to measure the baseline and ignored the resentment neighbors felt over the cash. GiveDirectly staff send about $40 every month to a phone SIM card. The recipients dodged bureaucratic surveillance and gained autonomy over job search decisions. Cash alters the job hunt. And they keep hunting.

This same technology could be integrated into GBI distribution.
